Built: every PRD requirement

Open a section to see each requirement by its PRD number.

1. Check-in and check-out7 of 7Built and tested›
  • CI-1Entrance QR code per location that changes every day at a set time; yesterday's code is refused. Staff get a full-screen "today's code" display.
  • CI-2Students scan with their own phone. A scan of today's code at the location counts as being there: the app never asks for the phone's location (v1.1).
  • CI-3Each check-in is tied to the student's schedule for that date, moved and added dates included; no date now means a clear message and no check-in.
  • CI-4The same code checks them out; a quick second scan asks before acting.
  • CI-5Automatic check-out at session end or closing time, whichever comes first, flagged for staff review.
  • CI-6Parents are notified at every check-in and check-out, with automatic ones labeled.
  • CI-7Live "In the building" list; staff check students in or out by hand, logged with their name and reason.
2. Schedules and periods5 of 5Built and tested›
  • SC-1Directors publish fixed schedules (for example, Mornings MWF 9:00–12:00) for a period: a month or a semester.
  • SC-2Seats per date; no seat assignment.
  • SC-3Periods and holiday closures. Periods at one location don't overlap; a closure cancels that date for every schedule and notifies enrolled families.
  • SC-4Editing or cancelling a schedule notifies enrolled families automatically. Once families are enrolled, its days are fixed.
  • SC-5Directors skip, add or move one date of one schedule; families are told, and check-in, the schedule and attendance follow the change (v1.1).
3. Enrollment and waitlists7 of 7, 1 retiredBuilt and tested›
  • EN-1A parent enrolls a student in one fixed schedule for its whole period; the student attends every listed date.
  • EN-2Only parents enroll, join a waitlist, switch or drop.
  • EN-3Retired in v1.1: students no longer request sessions; a parent picks the schedule.
  • EN-4Blocked when the membership is inactive or not valid on the schedule's first date, or when the schedule meets more days a week than the weekly limit.
  • EN-5A waitlist per schedule, with automatic enrollment of the first eligible student when a seat opens.
  • EN-6Parents can drop anytime, which frees the seat for the waitlist.
  • EN-7No penalties; a missed date simply shows as absent (closed, skipped and moved-away dates never do).
  • EN-8One schedule per period: enrolling in (or being moved into) another one in the same period switches, and the old seat goes to its waitlist (v1.1).
4. Membership status5 of 5Built and tested›
  • MB-1Status, weekly limit in days a week, start date and expiration date per student.
  • MB-2Staff enter and update memberships by hand; no payment data is stored.
  • MB-3Parents and students see status, cap and expiration.
  • MB-4Reminders 14 and 3 days before expiration.
  • MB-5On expiration, schedules pause rather than disappear, and come back on renewal if the seat is free and the days fit the limit.
5. Announcements and emergency alerts7 of 7Built and tested›
  • AN-1Directors post to their location: events, workshops, holidays, session offerings, news.
  • AN-2HQ posts to all locations or a chosen set.
  • AN-3Notifications plus an in-app feed; posts can be scheduled ahead.
  • AN-4Audience: all families, parents only, students only, or one schedule's families.
  • AN-5Emergency alerts: visually distinct, cannot be muted, reach everyone at the location.
  • AN-6Senders see delivered and read counts.
  • AN-7Directors (own location) and HQ end an emergency alert early: it stops being pinned, shows Ended, and everyone it reached gets an all-clear (v1.1).
6. Daily notes5 of 5Built and tested›
  • DN-1A short note for each student who attended that day.
  • DN-2The same four-item checklist at every location on a 3-point scale; HQ can edit the items. The note shows the student's goals for the day.
  • DN-3Optional comment, where staff also reply to the day's goals.
  • DN-4Written straight from the day's attendance list, ratings blank until set.
  • DN-5Parents are notified and can browse past notes by date, each with that day's goals; students don't see notes.
Daily goals (v1.1)3 of 3Built and tested›
  • DG-1After checking in, a student sets up to 3 goals for the day (a subject plus a sentence or two, with examples to start from) and can edit them that day.
  • DG-2Goals are optional: a student can skip a day. Staff see set, skipped or not answered for each student; parents see goals only in posted notes.
  • DG-3Every check-in asks for the day's goals until the day is answered, and the student Home card offers the same.
7. Attendance and hours export5 of 5Built and tested›
  • AT-1Every visit records in, out, total time, session and how check-out happened.
  • AT-2Parents view history by week, month or term.
  • AT-3Parents export an attendance and hours log as PDF or CSV for any date range.
  • AT-4Directors and HQ view and export reports by location, schedule and date range.
  • AT-5When a scheduled day passes with no check-in at all, each parent gets one notice 30 minutes after it ends (v1.1).
Users and roles6 of 6Built and tested›
  • RolesStudent, parent, location staff, branch director and HQ admin, each scoped to the right location.
  • FamilyA parent creates the family, invites each student by email or phone, and approves the link before the student can sign in.
  • GuardianA second guardian can be invited and sees the same records.
  • StaffDirectors manage staff accounts at their location.
  • HQHQ creates locations and assigns directors.
  • AccessFamilies see only their own data; staff only their location; HQ everything.

How it was checked

503 automated tests
Cover every requirement, each tagged with its number, plus cross-module flows for every v1.1 change; all pass.
1,403 deliberate breaks
The code was broken on purpose 1,403 different ways to prove the tests notice; every one was caught.
Hands-on walkthroughs and a full sweep
Reviewers used the app as student, parent, staff, director and HQ on phone and desktop sizes, then a 9-reviewer sweep re-checked everything, including privacy. More than 80 issues found and fixed; about 35 minor ones remain on the polish list.
Independent reviewers
Each part was checked by a reviewer who didn't build it, against the PRD and a leading childcare app as the quality bar.
