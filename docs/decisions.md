# Build decisions (v1 prototype)

The PRD leaves these open or unspecified. Each is a default chosen so the build
can proceed; any of them can be overturned by ELITE. Builders must follow them;
critics judge against them.

## PRD open questions

| # | PRD open question | Default in this build |
|---|---|---|
| D-1 | Can a student enroll at more than one location? | **Home location only.** Enrollment, check-in and notes all use the student's `users.location_id`. |

## Rules the PRD implies but does not spell out

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| D-10 | **Retired in v1.1.** Replaced by D-41 and D-42. *v1 text:* **A slot is (session, weekday).** "Mon/Wed/Fri 1–4pm" is one session with three weekly slots. Families enroll per slot, capacity is per slot, and the weekly cap counts slots. |
| D-11 | **Notifications.** Every notification goes into the in-app inbox (`notifications` table) and streams live to open apps. If the user has allowed browser notifications, they also get a system notification. Real Web Push (VAPID keys, HTTPS) is a deployment step and isn't in this prototype. |
| D-12 | **QR code.** The code value is an HMAC of the location's secret plus its current "QR day". The QR day changes at `qr_rotate_at` local time, so before 04:00 the previous day's code is still valid. The printed QR encodes a URL into the app (`/#/scan/<locationId>/<code>`), so a phone camera opens the app straight to the scan. |
| D-13 | **Check-in window.** **Changed wording in v1.1:** 30 minutes before a scheduled date's start until its end. Added dates count; skipped dates don't. *v1 text:* A student can check in from 30 minutes before an enrolled slot's start until its end. A scan outside every enrolled window is refused with a clear message (CI-3), and staff can override (CI-7). |
| D-14 | **Auto-checkout** (CI-5). The checkout time is the earlier of the slot's end and the location's closing time. The visit is flagged "Did not scan out" for staff review, and parents get a notification labeled as an automatic checkout. |
| D-15 | **Absent** (EN-7). **Changed in v1.1:** A past meeting date (D-47) of a live enrollment, not a closure, with no visit. Skipped and moved-away dates are never absent. *v1 text:* A past occurrence of an active enrollment counts as absent when it has no visit and its date isn't a closure or outside the term. It's computed, not stored, and carries no penalty. |
| D-16 | **Membership validity.** **Changed in v1.1:** Pausing and restoring work on whole-schedule enrollments. On renewal a paused schedule comes back if a seat is free, else the student goes to the front of its waitlist. *v1 text:* `expires_on` is the last valid day, inclusive. Once the location's local date passes it, or when staff set the membership inactive, the student's active enrollments become `paused` (MB-5). Renewing or reactivating restores them to `active` if the seat is still free; otherwise they go to the front of that slot's waitlist. Expiry reminders go out once each, at 14 and 3 days before. |
| D-17 | **Retired in v1.1** (D-48). *v1 text:* **Student requests** (EN-3) go to every active linked parent. The first parent to decide settles it. Approval then runs the same membership and capacity checks as a direct enrollment (see the flow diagram in the PRD). |
| D-18 | **Changed in v1.1.** Replaced by D-44. *v1 text:* **Waitlist** (EN-5) is per slot, first come first served. When a seat opens, the earliest waiting student who still passes the membership and cap checks is enrolled. Anyone ahead who fails a check is skipped but keeps their place. Joining the waitlist runs the membership and cap checks too. |
| D-19 | **Closures** (SC-3) cancel that date's occurrences. Enrollments stay. Families enrolled in any affected slot are notified. *v1.1:* Unchanged. Closures stay location-wide, next to the per-schedule changes (D-46). |
| D-20 | **Accounts** (AC-1/3). A parent signs up from the sign-in screen and picks a home location. They invite a student by email or phone, which creates a pending student with a one-time invite code. The student opens the invite and sets a password, and the parent then approves the link. Only after that approval can the student sign in. A parent can also invite a second guardian. |
| D-21 | **Staff accounts** (AC-4). A director creates staff at their own location with a temporary password, which is shown once. A director can deactivate staff at their location. HQ creates locations and directors (AC-5). |
| D-22 | **No payment data anywhere** (MB-2). Memberships carry status, cap and dates only. |
| D-23 | **Retired in v1.1 (moot).** No coordinates are ever sent or read (D-49). *v1 text:* **Location privacy** (NF-5). Scan coordinates are used for the radius check and then discarded. They're never written to the database or logs. |

Builders settled these while building their modules. They are this build's
defaults, like the rest of this file, and ELITE can overturn any of them.

| # | Rule |

| D-10a | **Seats per slot** (SC-2). **Changed in v1.1:** Seats per schedule (per meeting date), 1 to 1,000. Can't go below the students enrolled. *v1 text:* A session's capacity is a whole number from 1 to 1,000 seats per weekly slot. A director can't lower it below the students already enrolled on a day that stays. |
| D-15a | **A staff check-in with no session** (CI-7). **Changed wording in v1.1:** It counts toward any schedule it overlaps on one of that schedule's meeting dates. *v1 text:* It counts toward any session it overlaps at that location on one of the session's days in its term, closures aside (EN-7, D-15). It clears that day's absence in the schedule and in attendance, and a report filtered to that session (AT-4) lists the visit and its hours. |
| D-16a | **Renewal and the weekly cap** (MB-5, EN-4). **Changed in v1.1:** A paused schedule comes back only if its days a week fit the cap; otherwise it stays paused and saved. *v1 text:* When a membership is renewed or reactivated, paused slots come back only up to the weekly cap, counted the way enrollment counts it: active enrollments in active sessions of the current and every upcoming term. A slot that would go over the cap stays paused and saved, and comes back if staff later raise the cap. A slot whose seat was taken goes to the front of that slot's waitlist. Slots from a term that has ended are left alone. |
| D-24 | **Co-guardians** (AC-2, D-20). Co-guardians are direct only: two parents are co-guardians when one accepted the other's guardian invite. They are never followed transitively, and sharing a student does not make two parents co-guardians. A guardian who joins a family is linked to the inviter's students; their own existing students are not shared with the inviter. A new student invite links the student to the inviter and the inviter's direct co-guardians, and approving the student activates all of that student's pending links. |
| D-25 | **Posting a daily note** (DN-2, DN-4, DN-5). Ratings start blank and a draft may keep blanks. Post is refused until every checklist item is rated, and the message names the items still missing, so a parent never sees a blank score. Editing a posted note updates it in place with an "Edited" marker and an audit row, and does not notify parents again. |
| D-26 | **Shared contracts between modules.** **Changed in v1.1:** (4) now reads: "Whatever ends an enrollment sets `status = 'dropped'` and `ended_at`: a parent's drop, a switch (D-43), a cancelled schedule, a renewal whose seat was taken, the v1.1 migration." The day-removed path is gone (D-45). New (5): enrollments and waitlist rows use `weekday = 0` (D-42). New (6): meeting dates come only from `sessions/dates.js` (D-47). *v1 text:* (1) A membership change is written to `audit_log` with `target_type = 'membership'`, `target_id = memberships.id`, and `detail = { student_id, before: {status, weekly_cap, start_date, expires_on} or null, after: {...} }`. Attendance rebuilds when a membership was valid from these rows. (2) Every change to an enrollment's status is stamped in `enrollment_status_log` by a database trigger on the app clock, whichever module makes it. The enrollment schedule and attendance both read it to tell a paused week from an absent one. (3) Check-in writes notifications with kind `checkin`, `checkout` or `checkout_auto` and `ref_type = 'visit'`. A visit needs review while `flagged = 1 AND reviewed_by IS NULL`. (4) Whatever ends an enrollment sets `status = 'dropped'` and `ended_at` (a parent's drop, a cancelled session or removed day, a renewal whose seat was taken); a pause never sets `ended_at`. |

## v1.1 rules, from Stephen's answers and Justin's decisions (Oct 28, 2026)

The build plan is `docs/v1.1-design.md`. These rules override the defaults above where they differ; each
changed or retired rule above is marked "v1.1". D-11, D-12, D-14, D-20..D-22, D-24, D-25, D-29..D-32 and
D-34..D-38 are unchanged: wherever they say "slot" or "session", read "scheduled date of the schedule".

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| D-41 | **A schedule is a session row in a term** (S-3). It has a name, fixed weekdays, one 2-3 hour time, and seats per meeting date. Its period is its term: a month or a semester, as the director sets the term. The screens say "Schedule" and "Period". The code keeps `session` and `term`. |
| D-42 | **One enrollment row per schedule.** An enrollment or waitlist row with `weekday = 0` covers every meeting date of its schedule. Rows with 1-7 are v1 history and count only on their weekday. |
| D-43 | **One schedule per period, and the cap is days a week.** A student holds at most one live (active or paused) enrollment among schedules whose terms overlap (`a.start <= b.end AND b.start <= a.end`). Enrolling in another overlapping schedule is a **switch**: the old enrollment ends (dropped, D-26), its seat goes to its waitlist, and one notice tells the family about both. Enrollment is blocked when the schedule's `daysPerWeek` is more than the membership's `weekly_cap`. **Membership dates are checked against the schedule, not today:** let F be the schedule's first meeting date on or after today (D-47, closures removed). The membership must be `active` and `start_date <= F <= expires_on`. So a renewal starting Nov 1 can enroll on Oct 28 in a November period whose first date is Nov 2, and a membership ending Dec 31 can't enroll in a January period. Block codes and messages: `membership_inactive` "Leo's membership isn't active. Ask the front desk."; `membership_not_started` "Leo's membership starts Nov 1, after this schedule's next date (Oct 30). Choose a schedule that starts on or after Nov 1."; `membership_ends_first` "Leo's membership ends Dec 31, before January Mornings MWF starts (Jan 4). Renew it first."; `membership_expired` (already past) "Leo's membership ended Sep 30. Renew it first."; `no_membership` "Leo has no membership yet. Ask the front desk." A membership that ends partway through the period is allowed: the schedule pauses when it expires (MB-5). Schedules in periods that don't overlap (Fall, then January) can be held together. Why: Justin said a family picks ONE schedule per period, and then the cap check is a single comparison. |
| D-44 | **The waitlist is per schedule.** It is first come, first served, with renewals at the front (priority 1, D-16). When a seat opens, the earliest waiting student is enrolled if their membership passes the D-43 date check (against the schedule's first meeting date on or after the promotion day) and the schedule's days fit their cap. Anyone ahead who fails a check is skipped but keeps their place. A promotion is a switch if the student holds an overlapping schedule. Taking a seat removes the student's other waiting spots in overlapping periods, and the notice says so. A family can wait for a schedule while keeping its current one. |
| D-45 | **Days are fixed once families commit.** A schedule's weekdays can change only while it has no live enrollments and no one waiting. After that, the name, times (from today on, D-34) and seats can still change, and for single weeks the director uses date changes (D-46). To change days for the rest of the period, cancel the schedule and publish a new one. Why: families committed to that calendar, and the cap was checked against it. |
| D-46 | **Date changes, per schedule.** A director (own location; HQ any) can **skip** a meeting date, **add** a date that isn't one, or **move** a date (a skip and an add in one step, with one notice). The rules: dates today or later, by the location's date; inside the schedule's term; an added date isn't a closure day and uses the times the schedule has that day; a skipped date can't already have a visit for that schedule. Undo deletes the change, also today or later only. Every change notifies the students with an active or paused enrollment in the schedule, and their active parents, once. The weekly cap isn't checked against date changes: a week with an extra date is the director's choice. |
| D-47 | **What "meets on a date" means.** A schedule meets on date d when d is inside its term and either (d's weekday is one of its weekdays and d isn't skipped) or d is added. A closure at the location then cancels d for every schedule. Every module reads this from `sessions/dates.js` (D-26 (6)). |
| D-48 | **Only parents enroll** (S-3, EN-2). Students can't request schedules any more. The request routes, job, screens and notices are removed. `enrollment_requests` stays as read-only history, and old request items in the Inbox open Schedule. |
| D-49 | **No location check** (S-5). A scan of today's code at the student's home location counts as being there. The app never calls the browser's geolocation. The scan route ignores any `lat` and `lng` it is sent and never stores them. Location settings no longer show or accept a check-in radius, and new locations don't ask for coordinates (stored as 0, 0, 150). |
| D-50 | **Replaced by D-57 on Oct 28, 2026 (Justin).** *v1.1 text:* **Daily goals** (S-8). Each student has one goal record per location-local day: not answered (no row), set (1-3 goals), or skipped. A goal is a subject (Math, English, Science, History, Language, Art, Test prep or Other) plus 1-280 characters of text. Each subject offers three example goals the student can tap to start from. The student is asked after every check-in until they answer for that day. They can set or edit goals only on that day, and only after a check-in that day at their home location. Skip is allowed only while not answered; once goals are set they can be edited but not skipped. Staff see each student's goals and status on Daily notes. Parents see the goals inside the posted note only. Students see their own goals for today but still not notes (D-2). The subject list and examples are fixed in code (HQ can't edit them in v1.1). |
| D-51 | **Staff reply to goals in the note's comment** (DN-3). There is no separate reply thread on goals. Why: parents read one place, and staff write the note with the goals in front of them. |
| D-52 | **Ending an emergency alert early** (S-11). HQ can end any active alert. A director can end an active alert whose only location is their own, whoever sent it. An alert sent to several locations can be ended only by HQ, because ending it is network-wide. An ended alert is no longer pinned on Home or News. Everyone who can see it sees "Ended" with the time and who ended it (HQ shows as ELITE HQ). Its recipients get one normal-priority notice ("All clear: ... has ended"), sent even to people who muted announcements, because the alert itself ignored mute. An end can't be undone, and only alerts within their 24 hours can be ended. |
| D-53 | **Missed-day notice** (S-12). A student's day is missed when they had at least one scheduled date that day (D-47: not a closure or skipped date), each of those dates would count as absent (D-15: enrollment live and not paused when it began, membership valid), and there is no visit at all that day at their home location. Once the last of those dates has been over for 30 minutes (time for staff to fix a forgotten check-in), each active parent gets one `missed_day` notice for that student and date. A staff check-in made later doesn't take it back. The job only looks at each location's today and yesterday, so turning the feature on or seeding data never sends notices for old days, and the attendance seed writes placeholder rows (`notified = 0`) for its today and yesterday so a freshly seeded server sends only the one intended demo notice (5.3). A day where the student came to one session but not another is not "missed": it shows as Absent in Hours with no notice. |
| D-54 | **Retired Oct 28, 2026 by Justin:** no real families on v1; v1 demo databases are reseeded (docs/deploy.md), so nothing migrates v1 rows (MIG-1 retired too). *Design text:* **v1 enrollments move to whole schedules** at every app start (createApp), idempotently, as described in section 1.4. The migration never unseats a student over capacity (the seat count grows) or over the cap (the schedule is kept paused, D-16a), and never leaves a live waiting spot that could switch a student out of the schedule they kept. Its audit row lists every student affected. |
| D-55 | **Schema files: new tables and indexes only.** There is no `ALTER TABLE` (db.js re-runs every schema file on each open). New fields go in side tables. |
| D-56 | **Periods at one location don't overlap.** Creating or editing a term whose dates overlap another term at the same location is refused: 409 `term_overlaps` "Fall 2026 already covers Nov 2 - Nov 30. Periods at one location can't overlap. Use months or a semester for these dates, not both." Terms that already overlap in a v1 database are left as they are (the migration lists them) and must be fixed before either is edited. Why: with overlapping periods, "Switch to this" on a November schedule would end a whole Fall semester enrollment, and attendance's Period view, prev/next and "This term" (`termIndex`) pick one term per date. With no overlap, D-43's switch only ever replaces a schedule in the same stretch of dates, and attendance stays as it is. A location can still use months in one part of the year and semesters in another. |

## Weekly top 3 goals (Justin, Oct 28, 2026)

This replaces D-50 and the daily parts of DG-1..DG-3. D-51 (staff reply in the note's comment) stays. "3" comes from ELITE's board.

| # | Rule |

| D-57 | **Weekly top 3 goals replace daily goals (Justin, Oct 28, 2026).** *Why:* ELITE Spaces is a study hall for high-school homeschool and charter-school students in Los Angeles, and the app never knows their coursework. Goals are for accountability: mentors (staff, and parents acting as mentors) see what each student said they would work on and follow up, so nobody coasts. They also teach teens how to write a real goal. **The week** is Monday to Sunday by the location's date (NF-4). **Required, no skip:** at their first check-in of the week (after the check-in succeeds, never at a check-out) a student sets **exactly 3 goals**. Until the week has 3, the Check in screen and the student's Home card keep asking. **A goal** is a subject (Math, English, Science, History, Language, Art, Test prep, Other) plus one to three sentences: at least 6 words, at most 3 sentences and 500 characters. Shorter is refused with "Say what you'll finish: the chapter, the assignment or the problems.", the tip shown above the examples. Each subject shows one or two tap-to-use examples written for high schoolers (specific and finishable); tapping one fills the text box. Setting the week is one short flow, "Goal 1 of 3" to "Goal 3 of 3". **Three different subjects:** the 3 open goals each have a different subject; a repeat is refused with "Pick a different subject for each goal. Doing more than one thing in a subject? Put it all in that goal." Other counts as a subject and can be used once. **The daily update:** at every later check-in that week, each open goal is asked "Still working on this?" on its own: **Yes**, **Done** or **Change**. There is no button that answers all goals at once (Justin: that's a cheat). Done and Change both need the next goal (same rules), which takes the old one's place, so the week always has exactly 3 open goals. One answer per goal per day; answering again that day replaces it. It is asked once a day: once every open goal from an earlier day has today's answer, checking out and back in doesn't ask again. A goal added today isn't asked about until the next day. The next week starts fresh. The student can also answer or change goals from My goals (`#/goals`) with the same rules. Setting or answering needs a check-in that day at the home location (`no_visit_today`). When everything is answered the student sees "You're set for today". **Who sees what (NF-1):** a student only their own week; staff see each student's goals with that day's answer ("Still working on it", "Done today", "Changed today") or "No goals this week" on Daily notes (their location; HQ any); parents see each linked student's current week and every daily answer any time, read-only, from Home ("See updates"), and each posted note shows the goals as they stood that day. **Built with AI in mind (no AI code, vendor or data sharing now):** every goal text and Change text is judged by one function, `checkGoalText(text, { subject }) -> { ok, message }` in `src/modules/notes/goals.js`, used by every route that takes goal text (its message shows by the text box), so a later AI coach can replace or wrap it. The full dated history is kept and never deleted or overwritten: each goal's subject, text, created, done and replaced dates, `replaced_by`, and every daily answer with its date and time (`goal_weeks`, `week_goals`, `goal_checks` in `schema/070_notes.sql`), for a later counselor summary. AI is later, after privacy and legal sign-off (docs/open-items.md). The daily goal tables are gone: every database before this is demo data and is reseeded (docs/deploy.md). |
